How I can help

Promising research. Limited budgets. Important decisions.

Whether your asset is approaching first-in-human development or your Phase 1 results need to inform Phase 2, I provide focused scientific and operational leadership without requiring you to build a full internal team.

01 / DIRECTION

What evidence do we actually need?

Review the existing efficacy, PK/PD, biomarker and safety evidence; identify critical gaps; and create a prioritized, milestone-based development blueprint.

02 / EXECUTION

How do we deliver within budget?

Translate scientific questions into CRO scopes, compare technical capabilities and bids, support contract negotiations, and oversee studies, timelines and quality.

03 / DECISIONS

What should the data change?

Integrate results into clear recommendations on whether to proceed, biomarker plans, protocol considerations and submission-supporting scientific narratives.
